A useful first message can include:

- your name and organization, if applicable
- what you intend to build or commercialize with the project
- whether you are interested in the complete exclusive acquisition
- any technical area you want demonstrated

2. Clarification of acquisition scope and intended use.
3. Technical demonstration where appropriate.
4. Private source-code and architecture due diligence.
5. Agreement on price and transfer terms.
6. Formal exclusive transfer of the agreed project assets and intellectual property rights.
